# Changelog

## 0.2.0 — 2026-09-26

- Add **Home Theater room**: bind the card to a room from the Home Theater integration (0.2.0 or later) instead of the TV and receiver.
- In that mode, show what is playing with artwork, keep sources while the room is off, send arrow keys to the active player's remote, and link Configure to the integration's settings.
- Recognize Chromecast sources with a cast icon.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-09-26

- First release: one card per room for an LG webOS TV and an AV receiver.
- Power turns both devices on or off together; the TV turns on when a Wake-on-LAN automation exists.
- Favourite source chips and an All sources dialog switch both the receiver input and the TV input/app together.
- Arrow keys, OK, Back and Home for the TV; volume steps and mute for the receiver in dB.
- ARC check with a one-tap fix when the TV plays through its own speakers.
- Configure dialog with sound modes, TV sound output, device details and a Wake-on-LAN hint.
- English and Norwegian Bokmål card and visual editor; Default and Bubble appearances; color schemes.
